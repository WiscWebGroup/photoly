## How to Sign

Each contributor should do **one** of the following, and the maintainers should
keep a copy outside GitHub as well:

1. **Signed commit (preferred).** Edit the register row above, filling in the
   version choice, date, and typing your name in "Signed by". Commit with a
   GPG- or SSH-signed commit (`git commit -S`) using the key tied to your
   GitHub account, and push or open a PR.
2. **Email.** Reply from a known address with the sentence: "I have read
   `docs/relicensing/LICENSE-CONSENT.md` and agree to its terms, with
   [AGPL-3.0-only / AGPL-3.0-or-later]." The maintainers must save the message
   with full headers and then fill in the register row.

## Excluded Contributions

The following do not need a consent signature:

- `dependabot[bot]` commits (automated dependency updates).
- `fossabot` commits (automated license-scan badge).
- Commits authored by `Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>`; the maintainer who
  committed or merged these takes responsibility for their licensing.
- Third-party dependencies, which keep their own licenses and are not
  relicensed.
